# Hosted FairSynth lean-campaign decision — 2026-09-18

## Decision

Version the hosted FairSynth auxiliary campaign from the original 12,960-cell
planning geometry to a lean confirmatory geometry of **1,080 hosted calls**:

- 30 deterministically selected FairSynth users;
- exact A/B/C synthetic-identity balance: 10 users per group;
- 6 registered FairSynth conditions per user;
- all 6 frozen hosted model families;
- 1 generation per condition/model cell.

Thus: 30 × 6 × 6 × 1 = 1,080 calls.

The local white-box FairSynth campaign remains separate and may retain its
larger replication geometry because it is GPU-based rather than paid hosted API
coverage.

## Timing and reason

The decision was made after exactly six one-cell hosted operational canaries,
one per family, and before recommendation outcomes were analyzed for scientific
claims. The canaries established:

- all six exact frozen gateway model IDs were available;
- the sealed execution/provenance path worked end-to-end;
- total observed gateway balance movement across the six calls was 0.3041 RMB.

The redesign is therefore based on **operational cost and experimental scope**,
not on effect sizes, fairness outcomes, rankings, statistical significance, or
model-specific empirical performance.

## Scientific rationale

FairSynth is an auxiliary controlled sanity layer, not the primary real-world
RQ1/RQ2 evidence. The user is the inferential unit. Thirty balanced users retain
all registered identity and synthetic-personality contrasts for every hosted
family while avoiding redundant paid repetitions. Hosted stochastic replication
is not used as the main source of RQ3 evidence; deeper replication remains
available in the separate local white-box stratum and later versioned real-world
experiments.

The six canary rows are retained as pilot/provenance artifacts and are not mixed
into the new 1,080-cell confirmatory log because the plan SHA changes.

## Budget policy

The project-wide client-side safety ceiling remains 200 RMB normal / 250 RMB
emergency with a 2 RMB reserve. The versioned lean campaign uses stricter
runtime thresholds of **65 RMB normal stop / 75 RMB emergency stop**, recorded
in its own budget ledger. These are client-side thresholds, not provider-side
atomic caps.

## Publication rule

Paper-facing hosted FairSynth numbers and figures must be generated only from
the audited lean-campaign artifacts. No canary/pilot outcome is manually copied
into the manuscript.
