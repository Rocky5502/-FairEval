\textit{Protocol note.} One frozen Zhizengzeng OpenAI-compatible gateway provides hosted endpoint/key provenance but does not establish vendor-native reasoning or sampling semantics; those remain unverified unless returned metadata proves otherwise. A live model-list preflight is mandatory before paid execution. The project-wide client ceiling retains a 200 RMB normal stop target, a separate 250 RMB client-side emergency stop threshold, and a 2 RMB pre-cell reserve; this is not an atomic provider-side spend cap. The lean hosted FairSynth sweep uses stricter 65/75 RMB runtime thresholds and 1,080 planned calls (30 balanced users $\times$ 6 conditions $\times$ 6 families $\times$ 1 generation). The two open-weight models form a required but separately reported white-box stratum. Its canonical FairSynth run contains 12,960 generations (360 users $\times$ 6 conditions $\times$ 3 repetitions $\times$ 2 models). Local log-probability, NLL, perplexity, and margin diagnostics are uncalibrated and are never pooled with unavailable hosted internals.}
